How to Get the Most Out of Critical Fit

✦

The Big Idea

The goal of Critical Fit is to keep things simple yet fun while you begin your campaign to lose weight. The goal of this app is to keep you in a caloric deficit. We won't push you so much on what you eat as much as keeping your intake lower than what you use.

Weight loss is not all that difficult in theory. Only in practice. Many weight loss programs are gimmicky or macro-centric, selling some fad or trick. While we do suggest a healthy balance of carbs, fats, and proteins in your diet, mostly the app is meant to keep you using more energy than you store.

✦

Use It Daily

The key to get Critical Fit to help you is to use it… daily. Once you fill out your profile, Critical Fit will calculate how many calories your body burns each day if you actually just laid in bed all day (your basal metabolic rate). Then, using an industry-standard algorithm, it will adjust that number based on your usual level of activity. If you sit behind a desk all day, or if you work on a farm all day, or do something in between, that has an impact on how many calories you burn. The common term for this number is TDEE (total daily energy expenditure).

Every day you should log your weight, what you eat (Rations), and how much dedicated exercise you do (Activity). Critical Fit takes all of this and calculates if you are storing more calories than you burn… or burning more calories than you eat. The running result of this score is called your caloric deficit (Critical Fit calls it your Life Points). If you can maintain at least 500 Life Points at the end of each day, you should be losing at least 1 pound per week.

✦

Not Losing Weight? It's Not Working!

If you are consistently seeing more than 500 Life Points each day but not actually losing weight, here are the potential reasons.

  1. You are eating calories that are not getting logged. Make sure you log everything you eat.
  2. You are overestimating how much exercise you are actually doing. Don't log anything more than you are really doing.
  3. Your profile page has you as a higher activity lifestyle than you really have. If you do not have a blue collar job or are on your feet all day, you are most likely in the sedentary category.
  4. You aren't logging your actual weight every day. This is the most likely reason. Why is this important? Because the initial estimate of your caloric spend (TDEE) is exactly that… an estimate. The Life Points in the app are calculated off this number. However, because we are logging all your calories and your weight, we have all the data we need to be more precise. Logging daily will take that number from estimate to an observed pattern to a factual personalization. If you will log your calories and your weight into Critical Fit every day — whether the weight changes or not — for at least 7 days, your calculated data improves, and if you can log these for 28 days straight, we will know exactly how much energy your body burns. You can actually see this accuracy notated on your profile page near the bottom.

TL;DR

Log everything — including your weight — every day, and Critical Fit will customize itself to your personal information and become much more precise.
